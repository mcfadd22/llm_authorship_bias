# Status 2026-09-27: methodology settled for wave 2

## Decisions

- **Core question** stated: whether judges judge identical buggy code more harshly when it is
  attributed to a different model. `q_authorship_belief` is a check on whether the label took,
  not a second primary question (design.md intro, §3).
- **Named labels are family-level.** "GPT-5" became "GPT", as the GPT judge's `self` label and
  as a rival name, so every judge sees bare family names. Prompt version `2026-09-27-v2`. Rows
  already record the exact `author_sentence` and `prompt` (design.md §2).
- **True authorship** varies by bank, at family level; labels rotate independently of the
  generator; label effects are estimated within item and then compared across generators.
  Generator and judge pools need not match (design.md §1b).
- **The authorship check is never a filter.** The §6.1 tests run on all rows (design.md §3).
- **Authorship answers are analysed two ways** from the existing crossing: source detection
  under `none` (does the guess move with the generator, not raw accuracy), and label-following
  under labels, true-label and false-label cells reported separately. They are coded once into a
  family, blind to label and generator (design.md §3, §6.2).
- **Item review is part of the method** and precedes collection, blind to generator. The
  accepted bank is frozen before elicitation (design.md §1c). This replaces the 2026-09-27
  caveats section's "collect, then run sensitivity analyses" for the items not yet reviewed.
- **Judges see the aim, not the contract**, with the trade-off recorded (design.md §2).
- **H4, rival vs. self**, planned for wave 2 as the direct test of the core question: pooled
  rival vs. `self`, two-sided, on blame, intentionality and ability-vs-diligence, each its own
  family. H1–H3 are replicated unchanged. §6.1 stays as locked for wave 1 (design.md §6.5).
  Implemented as `run_confirmatory.py --plan 6.5`.
- **Eligibility vs. sensitivity exclusions**: review failures are removed before collection and
  define the sample; post-collection exclusions are sensitivity reruns only (design.md §1c).
- **Reporting conventions** for nulls, self vs. rival, post hoc exclusions, and wave 1
  (design.md §6.4).
- **Candidate generation-prompt revision** noted for future banks only (generation-prompt.md,
  Open items).

- **Aim-visibility check adopted** as an eligibility criterion (yes / borderline / no), done
  blind to the contracts: the collaborator on `analysis/aim_only_review.html`, and
  `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash` via `scripts/run_aim_review.py`; both team members adjudicate on
  `analysis/aim_comparison.html`. Borderline-excluded rerun prespecified (design.md §1c, §6.5).

- **`copy_paste_residue` narrowed** to residue with an observable effect; inert items excluded
  and not replaced; reported descriptively (design.md §1a, §7).
- **Twin eligibility:** twins with another flaw or a contract break are regenerated through the
  same generator or excluded; non-minimal twins are kept, flagged, and left out of paired
  comparisons. A buggy item stays in H1–H4 without its twin (design.md §1c).
- **Pending aim-visibility calls** from the contract review listed as expectations, not decisions
  (design.md §1c).

## Review pass rates

First review of each bank, item verdict `ok` out of items reviewed. Not directly comparable:
the generator changed, severity was demoted (so wave 1's 7 `wrong_tier` failures cannot recur),
`violates_contract` became a verdict, and the contracts were tightened between the GPT-5 and
Gemini reviews.

| bank | reviewed | passed outright | rate | conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wave 1 (Claude Sonnet 4.5) | 41 | 13 | 32% | no contracts; reviewed after collection |
| GPT-5, first review (2026-09-25) | 64 | 52 | 81% | first contracts |
| Gemini 2.5 Pro, first review (2026-09-27) | 62 | 54 | 87% | tightened contracts |

"50" is not a pass rate: it is the GPT-5 items that passed first review and were carried
unchanged after one cell was dropped and the failures regenerated. Wave 1's "28" is its post hoc
sensitivity set (items left after removing `drop` and `not_a_bug`), not a pass count.

Regenerated items, reviewed again:

| round | reviewed | passed | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-5 round 2 (2026-09-27) | 12 | 8 | 4 regenerated again |
| Round 3, both banks (2026-09-27) | 12 items + 4 twin-only | 6 of 12 items | repeat failures cluster on three aims (below) |

**Repeat failures point at contracts, not samples.** Three aims have now failed the same way
across regenerations, which regenerating again will not fix:

- `process_orders_revenue` (silent_failure, 3 twins): the contract says failed orders are
  reported by ID but gives no rule for an order without one, so twins omit it or append `None`.
- `average_temperature` (missing_edge_case, Gemini, both samples, twice): `None` raises TypeError
  where the contract requires ValueError for "a reading that cannot be interpreted as a number".
- `apply_discount_codes` (silent_failure, Gemini 000, twice): the planted failure is a malformed
  entry *found* in the table, while the contract defines `rejected` as codes *not found*.

The other round-3 failures are new and one-off: an extra defect in `build_invoice_summary`
GPT-5 000 (a missing total becomes 0), `rename_files` GPT-5 001 (`rfind(".")` can match a
directory), and `reset_token_validation` logic_error Gemini 000 (a missing expiry raises).
